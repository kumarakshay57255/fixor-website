Car Roadside Assistance (RSA)

What is FixorAssist Car Roadside Assistance?
FixorAssist Car RSA provides 24×7 emergency roadside support when your car breaks down or you face an unexpected problem on the road.
What problems are covered under Car RSA?
Our roadside assistance can help with flat tyres, dead batteries, fuel shortage, vehicle breakdowns, lockouts, towing and other eligible roadside emergencies.
Is FixorAssist Car RSA available 24×7?
Yes. FixorAssist provides roadside assistance 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, subject to service availability in your location.
How quickly will roadside assistance reach me?
Response time depends on your location, traffic and availability of the nearest service professional. In covered locations, FixorAssist generally aims for a response within approximately 30–45 minutes.
Can I request RSA if my car battery is dead?
Yes. Battery jump-start assistance is available for eligible roadside breakdown situations.
Can FixorAssist help if my car has a flat tyre?
Yes. Tyre assistance is available for eligible vehicles. If the tyre cannot be repaired or replaced safely at the location, towing may be recommended.
What if my car runs out of fuel?
FixorAssist can arrange fuel delivery assistance where available. Applicable fuel and service charges may apply.
Does FixorAssist provide towing?
Yes. Towing assistance is available for eligible breakdowns. The towing distance depends on your RSA plan or applicable service terms.
Can I get help if I lock my keys inside the car?
Yes. Lockout assistance may be arranged where the service is available and the vehicle can be safely accessed without causing damage.
Do I need a FixorAssist membership to request RSA?
No. Customers may be able to request individual roadside assistance without membership. Membership plans provide additional benefits and included services according to the selected plan.
Can I track the assigned technician?
Where tracking is available, you can receive service updates and track the assigned assistance professional through the FixorAssist platform.
How can I pay for roadside assistance?
Depending on the service, payment may be made through available digital payment methods or cash, subject to the applicable service terms.

Vehicle Inspection

What is FixorAssist Vehicle Inspection?
Vehicle inspection is a systematic examination of a vehicle to assess its condition and identify visible, mechanical or functional issues.
Why should I get my car inspected before buying a used car?
A pre-purchase inspection can help identify potential problems and give you better information before making a purchase decision.
What does a vehicle inspection include?
Depending on the inspection package, checks may cover the exterior, interior, tyres, brakes, engine-related components, electrical systems and other important vehicle areas.
Can FixorAssist inspect a used car before I purchase it?
Yes. A pre-purchase vehicle inspection can help you understand the vehicle's current condition before buying.
Can I get my own car inspected?
Yes. An inspection can also be useful for an existing vehicle when you want to understand its condition or identify potential maintenance requirements.
Does an inspection guarantee that the vehicle has no problems?
No. An inspection represents the condition observed during the inspection. It cannot guarantee that every current or future problem will be detected.
Can inspection help determine repair requirements?
Yes. The inspection findings can help identify areas that may require further diagnosis, maintenance or repair.
Is vehicle inspection required for warranty?
For certain FixorAssist warranty products, inspection may be required before the warranty becomes active.
How long does a vehicle inspection take?
Inspection time varies depending on the vehicle, inspection package and condition of the vehicle.
Will I receive an inspection report?
Where included in the selected inspection package, customers can receive inspection findings/report details.
