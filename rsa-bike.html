FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FixorAssist Roadside Assistance.

1.What does Roadside Assistance cover?

FixorAssist RSA is our on-the-spot breakdown support service. If your vehicle stops working on the road, our network gets you moving again — either through a quick fix at the spot or by arranging a tow to the nearest workshop.

2.What all is included under RSA?

Flat tyre change, battery jump-start, minor on-spot repairs, key lockout help, and breakdown/accident towing to an authorized workshop.

3.How fast will help reach me?

Typical arrival windows:
• City/highway locations: 60–90 minutes
• Difficult or hilly terrain: 150 minutes
• Remote/hard-to-access areas: up to 210 minutes

Actual arrival time can be affected by traffic, weather, road closures, or other conditions outside our control.

4.Is towing free?

Yes, up to 25 km from the breakdown/incident location. Beyond 25 km, distance charges apply as per prevailing rates.

5.Do I need to file a police report for towing after an accident?

Not for routine towing. For accident-related recovery, a Police Clearance Certificate is generally required before the vehicle can be moved.

6.Can someone else in my family use my RSA plan?

Yes — RSA is linked to the vehicle, not the driver. Any authorized driver of that vehicle can request assistance (key-unlock and towing may require valid ID verification).

7.Can I use one RSA plan across two vehicles?

No. Each plan is tied to a specific vehicle registration number. A separate plan is needed for each vehicle.

8.Is RSA available 24/7, pan-India?

Yes, RSA support is available round the clock across our serviceable locations nationwide.

9.What if I've used up all my free RSA incidents?

You can still request service on a pay-per-use basis, subject to availability in your location.

10.When would FixorAssist decline a service request?

If the driver is unlicensed, underage, intoxicated, involved in an unlawful act, or the vehicle is overloaded beyond permitted limits.

11.Can I cancel or change my RSA plan?

You can cancel anytime, though plans are non-refundable. Upgrades and vehicle-detail changes are allowed within 72 hours of purchase; downgrades are not permitted.

12.How do I request RSA assistance in an emergency?

Call our support line, or raise a request through the FixorAssist app/WhatsApp with your location pin — our team dispatches the nearest available service partner.

13.Will I be charged if the breakdown turns out to be something not covered under RSA?

If the issue falls outside your plan's coverage (e.g., a part replacement rather than an on-spot fix), you'll be informed of the applicable charge before any work is done.

14.What happens if I'm stuck somewhere with no network coverage?

Try reaching support via SMS or a shared location from someone nearby with network access. Where possible, keep our helpline number saved for quick access.

15.Can I track the service vehicle once it's been dispatched?

Yes, once a request is confirmed, live tracking of the assigned service partner is available through the app.

16.What if the assigned mechanic can't fix the issue on the spot?

The vehicle will be towed to the nearest authorized workshop within your plan's coverage terms.

17.Is RSA valid if I'm traveling outside my home city or state?

Yes, RSA coverage applies pan-India across our serviceable locations, not just your registered city.

18.Does RSA cover fuel delivery if I run out of fuel on the road?

This depends on your specific plan — check your plan document, as fuel-related assistance may be a separate add-on in some cases.

19.What documents do I need to keep handy when requesting RSA?

Your vehicle registration number, a valid driving license, and your RSA plan/subscription ID.

20.Can I request RSA for a two-wheeler and four-wheeler under the same account?

Each vehicle needs its own RSA plan; however, both plans can be managed from the same registered mobile number/account.

21.What if the breakdown happens due to an accident rather than a mechanical fault?

Accident-related towing follows the accidental recovery terms of your plan, which may have separate towing distance limits and documentation requirements compared to a routine breakdown.

22.How many free RSA incidents do I get in a year?

This depends on the specific plan tier purchased — refer to your plan document for the exact number of free incidents included.

23.Can I extend or renew my RSA plan before it expires?

Yes, renewal can be done through the app/website or by contacting support before your current plan lapses.

24.Will RSA assist if my vehicle breaks down at home or in a parking lot?

Yes, RSA coverage isn't limited to highways or open roads — assistance can be requested from any location, including your home or a parking facility.

25.What if I need a repeat visit for the same issue?

If the same issue recurs shortly after an on-spot fix, contact support — this is reviewed on a case-by-case basis as per plan terms.

26.Is there a limit on how many times I can use RSA in a month?

There's no fixed monthly cap, but usage is governed by the total free-incident limit and pay-per-use terms of your plan.

27.Can I transfer my RSA plan to a new vehicle if I sell my old one?

No, RSA plans are tied to the specific vehicle's registration number and are not transferable to a different vehicle.

28.What if I disagree with the charges applied for a service?

Raise a dispute with our support team along with your service request ID — it will be reviewed against your plan's terms.

29.Does RSA cover damage caused during towing?

Our network partners follow standard safety protocols; any grievance related to towing should be reported to support immediately for review.

30.Who do I contact for RSA-related complaints or feedback?

31.What is covered under "minor on-spot repair"?

Small fixable issues like loose connections, minor electrical faults, or adjustments that don't require spare parts or workshop equipment — anything beyond this is handled through towing.

32.Will RSA help if my vehicle's battery is completely dead and needs replacement, not just a jump-start?

A jump-start is provided free as part of RSA. If the battery itself needs replacement, that cost is borne by you, though our partner can assist with sourcing it.

33.Can I request RSA for someone else's vehicle if I'm driving it?

RSA is linked to the vehicle's registered plan, so assistance can be availed as long as the vehicle itself is covered, regardless of who is driving (subject to valid license and ID).

34.What if my car breaks down at night in an unfamiliar area?

RSA operates 24/7 — share your live location through the app or call our helpline, and the nearest available partner will be dispatched regardless of the hour.

35.Does RSA cover a flat tyre if I don't have a spare wheel?

Our technician will attempt a puncture repair on the spot. If no spare is available and the tyre can't be repaired, the vehicle may need to be towed for a replacement.

36.Is there a waiting charge if the technician arrives but I'm not at the location?

Extended waiting beyond a reasonable grace period may attract additional charges, similar to standard service norms — check your plan for specifics.

37.Can I choose which workshop my vehicle is towed to?

You can request a preferred authorized workshop; if it's outside our towing network or beyond the free-km limit, standard distance charges will apply.

38.What happens if the same vehicle breaks down multiple times in the same week?

Each valid breakdown is treated as a separate incident against your plan's free-incident allowance, unless it's clearly a repeat of an unresolved issue.

39.Does RSA include support if my vehicle is stuck due to flooding or waterlogging?

Assistance is attempted subject to safety conditions at the location; response may be delayed or limited during natural events like flooding, as these fall outside guaranteed TAT commitments.

40.Can I get RSA assistance for a rented or leased vehicle?

Yes, as long as the vehicle itself has an active RSA plan registered against its registration number, ownership status doesn't affect eligibility.

41.What if my vehicle needs to be moved from a no-parking zone due to a breakdown?

Our team will coordinate towing to a safe location or authorized workshop; any applicable fines from local authorities remain the vehicle owner's responsibility.

42.Is roadside assistance available for commercial vehicles?

Coverage depends on the specific plan purchased — commercial vehicle plans may have different terms than personal-vehicle plans, so check your plan document.

43.Will RSA help if I lock my keys inside the car and don't have a spare?

Key-unlock service requires a spare key to be available; if no spare exists, you'll need to arrange a locksmith or dealership support separately.

44.Can I get an estimated time of arrival before confirming my RSA request?

Yes, once your request is logged, an estimated arrival window is shared based on the nearest available service partner.

45.What if the RSA partner assigned to me cancels or doesn't show up?

Report this to support immediately — a replacement partner will be dispatched on priority, and the delay will be reviewed against your service TAT.

46.Does RSA cover damage that occurred before the breakdown, like pre-existing dents?

No, RSA only addresses the immediate breakdown/mobility issue — it is not a repair or damage-assessment service for unrelated pre-existing damage.

47.Can I add RSA coverage mid-way through my vehicle's ownership, or only at purchase?

RSA plans can generally be purchased anytime, not just at the time of vehicle purchase — check current eligibility with our support team.

48.What's the difference between breakdown towing and accidental towing limits?

Both currently offer free towing up to 25 km from the incident location; however, accidental towing may have additional documentation requirements like a Police Clearance Certificate.

49.Will I get a service report after RSA assistance is completed?

Yes, a summary of the service provided — including nature of issue and action taken — is available through the app once the request is closed.

50.Who is responsible if my vehicle is damaged during the towing process?

Our network partners are expected to follow safe towing protocols. Any damage during towing should be reported immediately to support for investigation and resolution.
