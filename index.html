ABOUT FIXORASSIST

About FixorAssist.
Driving Peace of Mind, Pan India.

FixorAssist is a multi-vertical automotive services platform built to be with you at every stage of your vehicle's life — from the day it's delivered to every service, repair, claim and breakdown that follows. Backed by 20 years of automotive industry experience, our team understands the everyday challenges vehicle owners face — and we've built FixorAssist to solve exactly those, with speed, transparency and a network that spans the country.

Whether you drive a car or ride a bike, FixorAssist brings together roadside assistance, servicing, insurance claim support, extended warranty and spare parts supply under one trusted roof — so you never have to juggle multiple vendors or wonder who to call next.

Our Verticals

Five services, one trusted platform — supporting your car or bike through its entire journey.

🚗🏍️

Roadside Assistance (RSA) — Car & Bike

Breakdowns don't wait for convenient timing, and neither do we. Our RSA network covers both cars and bikes — towing, on-spot repairs, battery jump-starts, fuel delivery and emergency support, whenever and wherever you need it.

🔧

Service & Maintenance

Regular care keeps your vehicle running longer and better. We deliver reliable, quality-checked upkeep — from routine servicing to preventive maintenance — through a growing pan-India workshop network.

📋

Insurance Claim Assistance

Insurance claims can be confusing and time-consuming. FixorAssist simplifies the entire process — guiding you through documentation, coordinating with insurers, and working towards a smoother, faster settlement.

🛡️

Extended Warranty — Up to 10 Years / 1,20,000 KM

Protect your vehicle well beyond the manufacturer's warranty. Our plans cover you for up to 10 years or 1,20,000 KM — long-term protection against unexpected repair costs, and complete peace of mind on every drive.

⚙️

Spare Parts Supply — Pan India

Genuine and quality-assured spare parts, delivered wherever you are. Our pan-India supply network ensures workshops and customers get the right part, on time, without compromising on quality.
