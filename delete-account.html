Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your FixorAssist Account

You can delete your FixorAssist account (customer app FIXOR and partner app FIXOR Partner) at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what happens to your data. FixorAssist is operated by Fixorauto Private Limited, New Delhi, India.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open the FIXOR app (or FIXOR Partner for vendors) and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from any address and we will delete your account for you:

Email: admin@fixorassist.com
Subject: Delete my FixorAssist account
Include: the registered mobile number and whether it is a customer or partner account.

We verify ownership of the number and complete deletion within 30 days of the request.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove your personal information, including:

  • Your name, mobile number, and email address
  • Profile photo and saved preferences
  • KYC / identity details (including Aadhaar/DigiLocker data for partners)
  • GSTIN and business details, if any

After deletion your account can no longer be used to sign in, and the mobile number is freed so you may register a fresh account later if you wish.

What is retained (and why)

Certain records must be kept to comply with Indian tax, GST, and accounting law even after your account is deleted. These are retained in de-identified form (not linked to your name/contact) for the period required by law, and are not used for any other purpose:

DataReasonRetention
Invoices & GST tax recordsStatutory tax / GST complianceUp to 8 years
Payment / transaction recordsFinancial & audit complianceUp to 8 years
